# Transition-stream change detection for Track-MDP — results (tabular, 2026-11-16)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-transition-dal-detector-design.md`. Code:
`src/core/transition_detector.py`, `src/core/probing.py`, `src/core/online_dal.py`.
Per-phase tables: `null_summary.md`, `sweep_summary.md`, `arms_summary.md`.

## What changed

The old "DAL" arms ran a GLR test on one per-step hit stream, pooled over every state and
generated by the learner. When nothing changes, that stream's misses cluster, so it
false-alarmed constantly (31-909 restarts for 14 switches). The new detector keeps one
Bernoulli stream per (cell i, next cell j). Only steps whose state is (i, tau = 0) feed it:
the object was just seen at i, so its next cell follows M[i, .] whatever the learner does.
That makes each stream i.i.d. between changes. Each stream runs a GLR test at delta_F / (number of
streams), with delta_F = 0.01. Coverage probes (rho = 0.05) top up streams the policy never
watches. On an alarm, the learner restarts at the end of that episode.

## Setup

Tabular SARSA (alpha 0.05, gamma 0.9, lambda 0, epsilon 0.1, optimistic 5), k = 5, sensor
cost -0.04, time_limit 1, 100-step episodes, 1000 episodes per segment. Environments: the
5x5 grid (105 streams) and the 10-node IoBT graph (41 streams). Matrices are the
random-matrix experiment's R0..R4 (Dirichlet(1) rows over stay + neighbours, seed 20261006).

## 1. Null check: no change, 1.5M steps per run, 3 seeds per env

| | Old hit-stream GLR | Transition streams |
|---|---|---|
| Alarms per run | about 25 at delta 1e-4 (one per 60,000 steps) | **0 in all 6 runs** |
| Ordered vs shuffled replay | ordered fires, shuffled never | match: 0-1 vs 0-2 alarms at delta_F 0.1 and 0.5 |

## 2. Detection: R0..R4 x 3 passes, 14 switches, monitor mode (learner never restarted)

Every probe rate tested (rho in {0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3}), in both environments, caught
14/14 switches with 0 false alarms. Delays were 0-4 episodes, median about 1 episode;
alarms are acted on at episode end, so the resolution is 100 steps. Free tau = 0 samples
alone are enough on this benchmark, because each switch redraws every row of M. I kept
rho = 0.05 so that a change confined to unwatched cells would still be seen; it costs under
0.3% of steps.

## 3. Accuracy: restart mode, 3 seeds, mean ± std (greedy evaluation)

| env | none | oracle cold | transition cold | oracle warm | transition warm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| grid | 94.64 ± 0.05 | 90.99 ± 0.17 | 91.24 ± 0.34 | **95.20 ± 0.12** | 94.97 ± 0.28 |
| IoBT | 94.73 ± 0.27 | 88.56 ± 0.34 | 88.48 ± 0.58 | **97.69 ± 0.06** | 97.64 ± 0.14 |

Sensors per step are 3.6-3.9 (grid) and 4.5-4.6 (IoBT) for every arm. Every transition run
caught 14/14 switches with 0 false alarms.

**Findings**

1. **Detection works.** The detector behaves like the oracle: in each (env, restart strategy)
   pair its accuracy is within 0.25 points of restarting exactly at the true switches.
2. **Whether to restart is a separate question from detection.** Cold restarts throw away
   learning and cost 3.4 (grid) and 6.2 (IoBT) points against never restarting, even when
   they happen at exactly the right time. Warm restarts to the equal-probability (C) base
   table gain 2.9 points on IoBT and 0.3 on grid. Faithful DAL/DARLING (cold restart) is the
   wrong choice for Track-MDP here; warm restart is the extension that pays.
3. **For comparison, the old hit-GLR warm arms** (single seed, `experiments/random_matrix`)
   scored 95.6% (grid) and 97.6% (IoBT) at this setting, with 71 and 47 restarts. Their
   accuracy was about the same because warm resets to C are cheap; the new detector gets the
   same accuracy with exactly one restart per real switch, and its alarms mean something.

## Limits and next steps

- Tabular only. PPO needs tau = 0 records gathered from the rollout workers, plus probe steps
  masked out of the PPO loss (spec item 7, not started).
- Every switch here changes every row, which is the easy case. A benchmark with localized
  changes (one or two rows) is what would exercise the coverage probes and rho.
- With field (IoBT audio) detections a "hit" comes from a classifier, so stream means are
  recall-scaled and noisier; this has not been tested.
- The sweep used one seed per rho.
